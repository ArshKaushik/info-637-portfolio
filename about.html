I'm a product designer & engineer with 4+ years of experience. I think in systems, prototype in code, and default to asking "why does this feel hard for the user" before touching Figma.

I care about design that holds up under pressure. The kind that works in messy startups and enterprise environments, adapts to real user behavior, and doesn't fall apart when the edge cases show up.

Outside of work: reading, cafe-hopping, photography, and trying to find the limits of whatever AI I'm currently poking at. Always up for a conversation about design, AI, psychology, or what's broken in the products we all use every day.
